Notes: Target duration ~75 min. Time budget: warm-up 12', activation 8', M1 25', M2 20', cool-down 5'. Use RPE 7 on the ramp-in and deload weeks; otherwise aim for RPE 7.5–8. Keep the lower-body accessory block crisp and technical.
